Case Study: Multi-Campaign Meta Ads Strategy – Lead Generation & Retargeting Performance

Client: E-commerce Brand (Confidential)

Campaign Duration: June 3 – August 20, 2024

Objective: Lead Generation, Retargeting & Conversions

Platform: Meta Ads Manager

Total Leads Generated: 933

Cost per Lead: $1.75

Checkouts Initiated: 283

Adds to Cart: 326

Avg. Cost per Checkout Initiated: $31.28

Avg. Cost per Add to Cart: $27.15

This Meta Ads account focused on building a full-funnel strategy combining lead generation and retargeting campaigns. A Lead Form campaign was used to capture high-volume, low-cost leads, while retargeting campaigns re-engaged warm audiences to drive purchase intent actions such as add-to-cart and checkout initiation.

The Lead Form campaign delivered strong results with 933 leads at a low cost, making it ideal for email and SMS follow-ups. Retargeting campaigns successfully converted engaged users into high-intent actions, proving the effectiveness of audience segmentation, creative testing, and budget optimization.

Case Study: High-Intent Application Leads via Meta Ads

Client: Service-Based Business (Confidential)

Campaign Duration: November 1–30, 2024

Objective: Lead Generation / Applications

Platform: Meta Ads Manager

Link Clicks: 496

Cost per Click (CPC): $1.20

Applications Submitted: 143

Cost per Application: $4.16

Total Leads: 140

This campaign focused on generating high-intent application leads for a service-based business using Meta Ads. Traffic was driven to a conversion-optimized landing page, ensuring clicks translated into real applications rather than low-quality inquiries.

With an efficient CPC of $1.20, the campaign produced 496 link clicks and converted 143 users into completed applications at a low cost per application of $4.16. This demonstrates strong audience targeting, clear messaging, and effective funnel optimization for lead quality.
